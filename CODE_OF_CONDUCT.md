# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Attack the change, not the person who made it. This repository has no
shortage of sharp criticism in it — in the docs, in review, in the issues — and that is on
purpose. What is not welcome is contempt, harassment, or anything that makes someone's
participation cost more than it should.

## One thing this project has that most do not

Loment is written by people and by AI agents, often in the same commit, and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says so on its first screen. Two consequences follow:

- **A contribution is judged on the change, not on what produced it.** "An agent wrote this" is
  not an argument, in either direction. Neither is "a human wrote this".
- **Do not use an agent as a shield.** Whoever submits a change is answerable for it. "The tool
  did it" is not a reply to a review.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, review comments, commit messages, and anywhere someone is representing
this project.

## Reporting

There is no committee here — one maintainer reads this. Use **GitHub's private reporting on this
repository** (*Security* → *Report a vulnerability*); it is the only private channel the project
has, and it reaches the maintainer and nobody else. Please do not open a public issue about it.

Reports are read, and they are taken seriously.
